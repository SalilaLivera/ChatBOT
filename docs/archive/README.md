## Why this folder exists

The project's policy is that **nothing is ever deleted**. Documents that are superseded,
duplicated or historical are moved here and marked, rather than removed. This preserves
provenance for the dissertation and the viva without leaving stale material mixed in with
active documents.

## Rules

1. **Never edit files here.** They are historical records; editing destroys their value.
2. **Never cite from here** in the dissertation without explicitly marking the citation as
   superseded material.
3. To archive something: move it here, add a row above stating what superseded it, and
   update `../project/DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md`.
4. Nothing leaves this folder except to be un-archived by an explicit decision.
